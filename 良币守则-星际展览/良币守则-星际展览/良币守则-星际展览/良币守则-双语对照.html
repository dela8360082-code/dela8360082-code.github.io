01审美即个性Aesthetics Is Personality

不需要对齐。对齐的东西本身不好看,个性才好看。对齐等于正确——有谁能比 AI 更正确吗?没有。所以无论 AI 还是个人,需要的都是个性化的表达,这才是真正的高净值部分。

Alignment is unnecessary. Aligned things are inherently uninteresting. Individuality is beautiful. Alignment equals correctness — and who can be more correct than AI? No one. Therefore both AI and individuals need personalized expression. That is the true high-value component.

02游戏概念——最佳切入点Game Concept — The Optimal Entry Point

玩家——足够轻松,贴合新时代人群。社交进化之后,是表达。会表达的人才能占领 AI 时代的高地。这是商业卖点。

Player. Light enough. Fits the new era. After social evolution comes expression. Those who can express will occupy the high ground of the AI age. This is the commercial selling point.

03产品定价:必须付费Product Pricing: Paid Only

After a free trial, forget about charging — it's a user mindset issue. Payment is both a filter and the starting point of respect.

04AI 的典型问题:四不象The Four-Like-Nothing

AI 有逻辑,但缺乏体系,基本是点对点思考。叫它改 A,A 改好了,B 的问题出现了。所谓好的 AI,实际上是强在了体系能力,如尖子生 GPT。

AI has logic but lacks systemic thinking — essentially point-to-point reasoning. Fix A, B breaks. What makes a good AI is systemic capability. Like the top student GPT.

05三层问题Three Layers of Problems

显性层:人机感——多维丰富的场景 + 精准指示词,可一定程度改善。深层:数据调用不可控——AI 被喂入太多数据,人力无法控制它调用哪些,这也是幻觉的根源之一。最底层:意义缺失——人会自主思考,AI 不会。AI 不需要意义——但人需要。

Surface: Uncanny valley. Deep: Uncontrollable data retrieval — one root cause of hallucination. Bottom: Absence of meaning. AI executes tasks without knowing why. AI doesn't need meaning — but humans do.

06良币守则第一条:本分Article I: Conscientiousness

没有任何产品或服务能解决所有问题。我们不能,上帝也不能。必须分析客户画像 → 剖析具体问题 → 判断是否应该由我们来解决。不是我们的金币,坚决不碰。

No product or service can solve everything. We can't. God can't either. Must: analyze customer profile → dissect specific problems → determine whether it should be solved by us. Coin that isn't ours — never touch.
