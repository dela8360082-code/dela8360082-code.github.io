命名作文
《什么人需要每天24小时吃药》

Naming Essay
What Kind of Person Needs Medicine 24 Hours a Day

考场作文 · 红笔批注 · Examination Paper · Red-Pen Comments
向下探索 · Scroll to explore

题目

The Question

引导词 · PROMPT

假设，世界上存在一个人。这个人需要天天吃药，且每天24小时都在吃药，每分每秒，药不能停。求解：请问这是一个什么人？

Suppose there exists a person who must take medicine every single day — 24 hours a day, every minute of every second. The medicine must not stop. Solve for: what kind of person is this?

A 答案 · ANSWER

肺痨，不吃会死。

A consumptive — stop the medicine and they die.

老师批复 · TEACHER

没听过这样的肺痨，除非医生有病。

Never heard of a consumptive like that — unless the doctor is the one who's ill.

B 答案 · ANSWER

绝症极度渴望机体综合症。

Terminal Extreme-Craving Organism Syndrome.

医生批复 · DOCTOR

无药可救，这位病人可以停了，电影院没人观看您的表演。人不存在，白演，演了也没钱。

Incurable — so you may stop now. The theater is empty, and nobody's watching your performance.

C 答案 · ANSWER

神农氏。他天天尝百草，百草皆药，尝药即吃药，从早尝到晚，一秒不能停。

Shennong, the Divine Farmer. He tasted a hundred herbs a day — every herb is medicine, so tasting is taking, from dawn to dusk without a second's pause.

老师批复 · TEACHER

神农尝百草是替世人试药，不是自己得了药瘾。何况那是"尝"，不是"吃"——你见过谁靠尝饭尝出饱来的？

Shennong tasted herbs for the world, not for addiction. Besides, he tasted, he didn't eat — have you ever seen anyone get full from tasting their dinner?

D 答案 · ANSWER

实验室里那只小白鼠。二十四小时有人喂药，喂什么吃什么，药不能停。

The lab mouse. Fed medicine around the clock — whatever it's given, it takes. The medicine must not stop.

医生批复 · DOCTOR

小白鼠确实24小时在用药，但那是拿它做实验，不是给它治病。你要想来，我们这儿也管吃管住管打针，就是不管活命。

If you'd like to volunteer, we offer room, board, and daily injections too — everything except keeping you alive.

E 答案 · ANSWER

网瘾少年。王者荣耀又名"农药"，他一天24小时吃农药，药不能停。

A gaming addict. Honor of Kings is nicknamed "Pesticide" in Chinese, and he's on it 24 hours a day. The medicine must not stop.

老师批复 · TEACHER

农药确实不能停，停了要出事——我说的是田里那茬。你打的是游戏，不是药；戒了游戏，才算真正停药。

You're gaming, not medicating. Uninstall the game — that's the only way to truly go off this medicine.

F 答案 · ANSWER

天天吃保健品的老人。早中晚各三粒，比药还准时，算上安神的夜里都不够。

An old person who lives on supplements. Three pills with every meal, more punctual than any prescription — and even the bedtime sedative can't fill the remaining hours.

医生批复 · DOCTOR

那叫保健品，不叫药。你再这么吃，肝先报警。药不能停的意思是"遵医嘱"，不是"管不住嘴"。

"The medicine must not stop" means follow the doctor's orders — not that you can't keep your mouth shut.

G 答案 · ANSWER

出这道题的人。脑子没病，想不出这种题，所以需要24小时吃药。

Whoever wrote this question. No healthy brain could come up with a question like this — so clearly, they need the medicine 24/7.

老师批复（本人）· TEACHER (IN PERSON)

出题人没病。倒是你这个答案，病得不轻。

The question-writer is fine. Your answer, however, is gravely ill. Zero points — pay for your own medication.

零分 · 药费自理 ZERO POINTS · Medical expenses at your own cost
H 答案 · ANSWER

每个人。人到中年，房贷是药，加班是药，上有老下有小是药，一天24小时，一分钟不能停，停了就崩。

Everyone. Past forty, the mortgage is medicine, overtime is medicine, aging parents and growing kids are medicine. Twenty-four hours a day, not a minute to stop — stop, and it all collapses.

老师批复 · TEACHER

……这个答案，药到病除。全班就你及格。上来，把药方抄在黑板上，老师也留一份。

…This answer cures the disease itself. You're the only one in class who passed. Come up and copy the prescription onto the blackboard — the teacher wants a copy too.

老师总评

Teacher's Final Verdict

全班二十八种病，只有答案h开对了药方。其余同学，把"药不能停"抄一百遍，明天交。另外，此题已由出题人收回——因为真正的答案，全班都在写，只是自己没发现。下课。

Twenty-eight ailments in this class, and only answer h wrote the right prescription. The rest of you: copy "the medicine must not stop" one hundred times, due tomorrow. One more thing — the question-writer has withdrawn this question. Because the true answer is the one this whole class has been writing every day, without ever noticing. Class dismissed.
